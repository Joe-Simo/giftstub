Adding people and dates

On the People tab, tap + and enter a name, and add a birthday if you know it. The year is optional, and a February 29 birthday is remembered on February 28 in other years. Tap Add a Date for anniversaries or any other yearly date.

Gift ideas

Open a person, tap +, then Add Gift Idea. Each gift moves from idea to bought to wrapped to given. In a list, swipe a gift to move it to the next step. If a new idea looks like something you already gave that person, Giftstub warns you.

Gift lists and budgets

On the Lists tab, start a list such as Christmas and set an overall budget. The list shows what you have spent, what your ideas add up to, and what is left, with To Buy and To Wrap filters. The free version has one active list. With Giftstub Pro you can run as many lists as you like and set a budget for each person on a list.

Reminders

Giftstub reminds you on the day and a week before each birthday, date, and list, at the hour you pick in Settings. With Giftstub Pro, choose a heads-up of 1, 3, 7, 14, or 30 days instead. If reminders don't arrive, open Settings on your iPhone, then Notifications, then Giftstub, and turn on Allow Notifications.

Widgets

With Giftstub Pro, touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Giftstub. Coming Up counts down to the next birthdays, dates, and lists. Gift Budget shows what you have spent on your next list and what is left.

Backup and export

In Settings, Back Up Everything creates a file you can save to Files or send to yourself. Restore from Backup reads that file and adds anything that is not already in Giftstub, so restoring twice does not create duplicates. Export Gifts as CSV creates a spreadsheet of your gifts.

Purchases

Giftstub Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If Pro isn't unlocked after buying, open Settings in Giftstub and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account. For refund requests, visit Apple's purchase support.

Your data

Giftstub keeps your people, gifts, and lists only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes its data, so back up first if you want a copy. See the Privacy Policy.
